## 摘要
随着数字文档交换的增长，保护图像完整性以抵御向量量化（VQ）和拼贴等攻击变得至关重要。现有方法容易受到这些攻击的影响，并且局限于固定图像尺寸。本文提出了一种新颖的、与维度无关的脆弱水印算法，通过将传统的哈希函数替换为三角内容感知置换（TCA），增强了安全性和篡改定位能力。图像与基于密钥的全局噪声结合后被分割成块。核心创新在于在位平面级别应用具有内在雪崩效应的内容相关置换（TCA），生成独特的基于内容的水印。对于彩色图像，垂直三明治变换合并通道，在仅增加1.62倍时间的情况下保持通道间依赖性。"余数合并"策略消除了填充约束。在50张灰度图像和10张彩色图像上进行的实验表明，在18种攻击中17种攻击的FPR=0%且FNR=0%。椒盐噪声下的FNR分别为0.27%（灰度）和0.14%（彩色）。平均PSNR分别为51.14 dB（8位）、75.25 dB（12位）和99.33 dB（16位）。嵌入和提取时间分别为1.61秒和1.63秒。该算法在对抗拼贴、VQ、复制移动、JPEG（质量5-95）和几何攻击时达到100%准确率，为数字取证、医学成像和法律文件认证提供了安全解决方案。

## Abstract
With the growth of digital document exchange, protecting image integrity against attacks such as Vector Quantization (VQ) and collage has become critical. Existing methods are vulnerable to these attacks and limited to fixed image dimensions. This paper presents a novel, dimension-agnostic, fragile watermarking algorithm that enhances security and tamper localization by replacing conventional hash functions with Triangular Content-Aware Permutation (TCA).   The image is combined with key-based global noise and divided into blocks. The core innovation is applying content-dependent permutation with intrinsic avalanche effect (TCA) at the bit-plane level, generating a unique content-dependent watermark. For color images, a vertical sandwich transformation merges channels, preserving inter-channel dependency with only 1.62x time increase. The "remainder merging" strategy eliminates padding constraints.   Experiments on 50 grayscale and 10 color images under 18 attacks show FPR=0% and FNR=0% for 17 attacks. Salt-and-pepper noise yields negligible FNR of 0.27% (grayscale) and 0.14% (color). Average PSNR is 51.14 dB (8-bit), 75.25 dB (12-bit), and 99.33 dB (16-bit). Embedding and extraction times are 1.61 s and 1.63 s, respectively.   The algorithm achieves 100% accuracy against collage, VQ, copy-move, JPEG (quality 5-95), and geometric attacks, providing a secure solution for digital forensics, medical imaging, and legal document authentication.
