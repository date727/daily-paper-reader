## 摘要
语义变化检测（SCD）是一项双时态密集预测任务，旨在联合识别变化区域及其变化前后的语义状态。与单图像分割或二元变化检测不同，SCD将两个时态输入与按时间戳的语义预测、变化定位以及最终的语义变化解码相结合，从而产生传统鲁棒性协议未涵盖的对抗依赖关系。我们提出了一种任务特定的评估框架，将输出端攻击目标与输入端时态扰动访问分离，从而实现对组件脆弱性和跨时态传播的系统分析。在四个数据集和六种代表性CNN、Transformer及状态空间模型上的实验评估了组件级和时态目标、单一时态和双时态扰动、多种攻击方法以及跨架构迁移性。结果表明，即使二元变化定位相对稳定，最终的语义变化预测仍可能被严重破坏，并且某一时间戳相关的扰动或攻击目标可能传播至另一时间戳的预测结果。这些行为在不同架构家族中普遍存在，而直接跨模型迁移的效果仍显著弱于白盒攻击。本研究证明了SCD中的对抗鲁棒性依赖于完整的双时态预测路径，而非单个分支或主干架构家族，并提供了针对耦合双时态图像分析的结构化鲁棒性评估协议。代码可在https://github.com/EricYu97/AdvSCD获取。

## Abstract
Semantic change detection (SCD) is a bitemporal dense-prediction task that jointly identifies changed regions and their semantic states before and after change. Unlike single-image segmentation or binary change detection, SCD couples two temporal inputs with timestamp-wise semantic prediction, change localization, and final semantic-change decoding, creating adversarial dependencies that are not captured by conventional robustness protocols. We present a task-specific evaluation framework that separates output-side attack objectives from input-side temporal perturbation access, enabling systematic analysis of component vulnerability and cross-temporal propagation. Experiments on four datasets and six representative CNN-, Transformer-, and state-space-based models evaluate component-level and temporal objectives, single- and dual-timestamp perturbations, multiple attack methods, and cross-architecture transferability. The results show that final semantic-change predictions can be severely corrupted even when binary change localization remains comparatively stable, and that perturbations or attack objectives associated with one timestamp can propagate to the prediction of the other. These behaviors occur across different architecture families, while direct cross-model transfer remains considerably weaker than white-box attacks. The study demonstrates that adversarial robustness in SCD depends on the complete bitemporal prediction pathway rather than on an individual branch or backbone family, and provides a structured protocol for evaluating robustness in coupled bitemporal image analysis. Code is available at https://github.com/EricYu97/AdvSCD.
