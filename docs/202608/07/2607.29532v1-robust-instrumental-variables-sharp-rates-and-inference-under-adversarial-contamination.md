## 摘要
由于2SLS基于样本均值，少量观测值可能对估计和推断产生不成比例的影响。我们引入了W-2SLS，这是一种简单的稳健化方法，通过将这些均值替换为分位数截尾均值来实现。我们分析了W-2SLS在对抗性污染下的表现，这种污染允许被污染观测值的身份和报告值依赖于实际的干净样本，因此能够容纳有针对性或策略性的操控。在有限m阶矩条件下，W-2SLS达到最小最大精确速率η_{n}^{1-\frac1m}+n^{-1/2}，其中η_n是可能被修改的观测值比例。匹配的下限界确定了与无污染样本的2SLS具有相同首一阶规律的均匀一致性、根n估计和中心高斯推断的确切污染阈值。当\sqrt{n}η_{n}^{1-\frac1m}\to 0时，稳健性具有首一阶免费性质。我们还构建了可行的异方差稳健推断方法以及在弱识别和对抗性污染下有效的截尾安德森-鲁宾检验。最后，即使没有污染，普通2SLS也可能在有限样本下表现出较差的均匀集中性，而W-2SLS则允许置信校准的次高斯偏差保证。

## Abstract
Because 2SLS is built from sample averages, a small number of observations can have a disproportionate effect on estimates and inference. We introduce W-2SLS, a simple drop-in robustification that replaces these averages by quantile-winsorized means. We analyze W-2SLS under adversarial contamination, which permits both the identities and the reported values of the contaminated observations to depend on the realized clean sample and therefore accommodates targeted or strategic manipulation. Under finite $m$-th moments, W-2SLS attains the minimax-sharp rate $η_{n}^{1-\frac1m}+n^{-1/2}$, where $η_n$ is the fraction of observations that may be altered. Matching lower bounds identify the exact contamination thresholds for uniform consistency, root-$n$ estimation, and centered Gaussian inference with the same first-order law as clean-sample 2SLS. When $\sqrt{n}η_{n}^{1-\frac1m}\to 0$ robustness is first-order free. We also construct feasible heteroskedasticity-robust inference and a winsorized Anderson--Rubin test valid under weak identification and adversarial contamination. Finally, even without contamination, ordinary 2SLS can have poor uniform finite-sample concentration, whereas W-2SLS admits confidence-calibrated sub-Gaussian deviation guarantees.
