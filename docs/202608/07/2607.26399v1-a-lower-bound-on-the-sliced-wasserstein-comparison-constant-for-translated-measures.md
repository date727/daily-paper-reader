## 摘要
我们为实数空间ℝ^d上的平移概率测度提供了标准1-Wasserstein距离（W₁）和切片1-Wasserstein距离（SW₁）的闭式表达式。此外，我们为卡莱尔、菲加利、梅里戈和王在ℝ^d上的平移概率测度之间建立的W₁与SW₁之间的常数c_d构造了一个显式下界[1]。切片Wasserstein距离计算效率高，并被用于图像生成等其他应用，这引发了计算效率与理论正确性之间的自然问题。对常数c_d的下界提供了关于该效率成本的信息。

## Abstract
We provide closed-form expressions for standard 1-Wasserstein distance ($W_1$) and Sliced 1-Wasserstein distance ($SW_1$) for translated probability measures on $\mathbb{R}^d$. Furthermore, we construct an explicit lower bound for the constant $c_d$ established by Carlier, Figalli, Mérigot, and Wang between $W_1$ and $SW_1$ [1] for translated probability measures on $\mathbb{R}^d$. Sliced Wasserstein distances are computationally efficient and used in image generation and other applications, which raises natural questions in computational efficiency versus theoretical correctness. A lower bound on the constant $c_d$ gives information about the cost of that efficiency.
