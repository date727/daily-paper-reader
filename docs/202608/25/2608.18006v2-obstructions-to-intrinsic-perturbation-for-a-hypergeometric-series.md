## 摘要
我们证明，在$A$-超几何系统的一个伪指数点以及对于有序负支持族的情况下，内在扰动在$\ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A)$内可以产生比环境扰动更小的系数空间，从而回答了Okuyama--Saito的问题。我们通过一个内在扰动障碍模来衡量这种差距，该模是两个余子理想的商，其分级对偶是环境系数空间模内在系数空间。我们通过涉及$\operatorname{Tor}_1$的两个右精确序列来实现该模，并用两个反链有限地表示它。对于同质系统，我们将规范形式解空间与通过所有在该空间中出现的指数和所有有序负支持族得到的内在扰动规范级数的张成空间的商表示为有限维余核，并计算该张成空间的余维数；该表示和余维数转移到共同非奇异域上的全纯解空间。我们给出了格秩为1和2且具有非零障碍的配置，其中一个配置具有有序区分集合并解决了Okuyama--Saito未解决的情况。对于每个整数$q\geq 1$，我们给出了一个格秩为2、具有连通列拟阵且由约化Gröbner基向量生成的正规仿射半群的配置，其障碍模的维度为$q^2$，并且通过内在扰动得到的规范级数之和张成的子空间在全纯解空间中的余维数至少为$\lceil 3q^2/4\rceil$，因此在固定格秩下该余维数是无界的。

## Abstract
We show that, at a fake exponent of an $A$-hypergeometric system and for an ordered negative support family, intrinsic perturbation within $\ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(A)$ can produce a strictly smaller coefficient space than ambient perturbation, which answers a question of Okuyama--Saito. We measure the gap by an intrinsic-perturbation obstruction module, a quotient of two colon ideals whose graded dual is the ambient coefficient space modulo the intrinsic one, and we realize this module by two right-exact sequences involving $\operatorname{Tor}_1$ and present it finitely by two antichains. For a homogeneous system, we present as a finite-dimensional cokernel the quotient of the canonical formal solution space by the span of the canonical series obtained by intrinsic perturbation, taken over all exponents occurring in that space and all ordered negative support families, and we compute the codimension of that span; the presentation and the codimension transfer to the holomorphic solutions on a common nonsingular domain. We give configurations of lattice rank 1 and 2 with nonzero obstruction, one of which has an ordered distinguished collection and settles the case left open by Okuyama--Saito. We give, for every integer $q\geq 1$, a configuration of lattice rank 2 with a connected column matroid and with a normal affine semigroup generated by the reduced Gröbner basis vectors, whose obstruction module has dimension $q^2$ and for which the sums of the canonical series obtained by intrinsic perturbation span a subspace of the holomorphic solution space of codimension at least $\lceil 3q^2/4\rceil$, so this codimension is unbounded at fixed lattice rank.
