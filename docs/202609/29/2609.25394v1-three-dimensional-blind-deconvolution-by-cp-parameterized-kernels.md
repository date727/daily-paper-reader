## 摘要
我们考虑从卷积数据 $g = k*u$ 中重建核函数 $k$ 和图像 $u$ 的盲去卷积问题。我们特别关注三维情况，即体积图像。该问题通过为核函数施加半参数化CP分解，并为图像施加变分TV正则化来解决。我们还讨论了运动模糊歧义效应，即在盲去卷积问题中可能出现于某些运动物体视频中的核函数非唯一性问题。盲去卷积算法采用交替最小化方法对 $u$ 进行TV正则化，并对核函数进行CP分解步骤。这些步骤通过正则化投影、核归一化以及因果支持投影实现。

## Abstract
We consider the problem of reconstructing a kernel $k$ and image $u$ in a blind deconvolution problem from convolutional data $g = k*u$. We particularly focus on the 3D case, i.e., on volumetric images. The problem is approached by imposing a semiparametric CP decomposition for the kernel and a variational TV penalty for the image. We also discuss a motion blur ambiguity effect, i.e., a nonuniqueness issue of the kernel in the blind deconvolution problem that may appear in certain videos of moving objects. The blind deconvolution algorithm uses an alternating minimization approach with TV regularization for $u$ and a CP decomposition step for the kernel. These are implemented with positivity projection, kernel normalization, and\textbackslash or causal support projection steps. Several numerical examples for 3D MRI images, hyperspectral images, and a grayscale test video show the effectiveness of the method.
