## 摘要
双摄像头数字变焦转换通常会表现出几何结构和色一致性的明显不连续性，从而降低用户体验。尽管近期的双摄像头平滑变焦（DCSZ）方法尝试通过在DCSZ数据上微调帧插值（FI）模型来缓解这一问题，但它们在处理跨视角差异较大和复杂的几何变换时仍存在困难。考虑到扩散模型的生成先验适合解决此问题，我们探索其在DCSZ中的应用。然而，直接应用现有的基于扩散的FI模型仍会导致低保真度的转换，原因包括条件引导不足、VAE编码过程中高频信息损失以及时间一致性不足。为此，我们提出ZoomDiff，一种利用双摄像头输入在潜在空间和像素空间中进行高保真度扩散建模的方法，以实现照片级真实的转换。具体而言，我们首先在多步去噪过程中增强双图像条件引导以提升几何一致性；随后将VAE编码器中提取的流对齐多尺度特征注入到VAE解码器中以恢复高频细节，并引入流引导的时间一致性监督以生成更平滑的转换。在合成数据集和真实世界数据集上的大量实验表明，ZoomDiff在定量和定性上均优于现有最先进的方法。项目页面：https://jiayi-hit.github.io/ZoomDiff.github.io/

## Abstract
Digital zoom transitions between dual cameras often exhibit conspicuous discontinuities in geometric structure and chromatic consistency, degrading the user experience. While recent dual-camera smooth zoom (DCSZ) methods attempt to mitigate this by fine-tuning frame interpolation (FI) models on DCSZ data, they struggle with the large cross-view disparities and complex geometric transformations. Considering that the generative prior of diffusion models is suitable for addressing this problem, we explore their application to DCSZ. However, naively applying existing diffusion-based FI models still yields low-fidelity transitions due to insufficient conditional guidance, high-frequency information loss during VAE encoding, as well as inadequate temporal consistency. To address this, we propose ZoomDiff, a high-fidelity diffusion model that leverages dual-camera inputs in both latent and pixel spaces for photo-realistic transitions. Specifically, we first strengthen dual-image conditional guidance during the multi-step denoising process to improve geometric consistency. Then we inject flow-aligned multi-scale features from the VAE encoder into the VAE decoder to recover high-frequency details, where flow-guided temporal consistency supervision are introduced to produce more smooth transitions. Extensive experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that ZoomDiff outperforms state-of-the-art methods quantitatively and qualitatively.
