## 摘要
我们研究了在有限矩假设和对抗性污染下任意阶简单随机张量的稳健估计问题。我们提出了首个在该设定下实现近优无维统计速率的稳健估计器。当$p\ge2q$矩存在时，该估计器达到近优的污染率，并且在整个弱矩范围$q\le p\le2q$内仍能提供非平凡保证。基于方向截尾均值和最小最大聚合方法，我们的估计器能够自适应地处理$p$并无需依赖区间交集程序即可对超合同常数的上界进行控制。我们的分析将截尾均值框架扩展到任意阶张量，并特别建立了高阶计数和截断经验多向量乘积过程的集中不等式。我们认为这些不等式在当前应用之外也具有独立的研究价值，包括算法稳健估计领域。

## Abstract
We study robust estimation of simple random tensors of arbitrary order $q\in\mathbb{N}$ under finite-moment assumptions and adversarial contamination. We propose the first robust estimator achieving near-optimal dimension-free statistical rates in this setting. The estimator attains the near-optimal corruption rate whenever $p\ge2q$ moments are finite and continues to provide nontrivial guarantees throughout the weak-moment regime $q\le p\le2q$. Being based on directional trimmed means and minimax aggregation, our estimator is adaptive to $p$ and upper bounds on hypercontractive constants without resorting to interval-intersection procedures. Our analysis extends the trimmed-mean framework underlying recent advances in robust mean and covariance estimation to arbitrary tensor order. In particular, we establish concentration inequalities for higher-order counting and truncated empirical multi-vector product processes. We believe these inequalities could be of independent interest beyond the present application, including algorithmic robust estimation.
