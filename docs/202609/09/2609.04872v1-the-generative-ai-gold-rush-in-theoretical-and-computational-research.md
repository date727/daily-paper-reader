## 摘要
生成式人工智能正在改变理论与计算研究的生产条件，但其系统层面的影响需要采取措施来区分平台增长、领域特定差异和生产结构。我们收集了从2018年1月至2026年8月期间，针对二十个arXiv存档库的2,080个月度观测数据，并建立了一个独立的伪匿名化数学作者面板。通过2025年12月拟合的正则化凸合成控制法识别出2026年1月至8月的异常现象，同时采用空间安慰剂、前一年伪保留期、供体重新拟合和替代预时期等方法评估比较稳健性。数学领域记录了47,127条列表条目，比2025年增长33.5%，且比合成反事实的42,113条条目高出11.9%。合格的供体和预时期设计分别产生9.6%至14.9%的增长，数学领域在十五个符合条件的安慰剂存档库中具有最大的均方根预测误差比率。次领域增长广泛，30个主要math.*分类中有29个出现扩张。作者面板显示重复输出尾部显著增厚。至少提交五次的活跃作者单位占比从2.45%上升至3.80%，而十次提交尾部占比从0.21%上升至0.49%。这些结果记录了2026年数学领域出现的新且异常显著的生产制度转变。其时间点和生产结构，结合独立的人工智能扩散证据和可验证的研究任务，与延迟扩散和能力阈值机制一致。比较设计识别了异常现象，而独立三角验证评估了与人工智能相关的解释。研究结果将验证、选择和注意力确定为研究治理的核心约束条件。

## Abstract
Generative AI is changing the production conditions of theoretical and computational research, but its sys tem level effects require measures that separate plat form growth, field specific divergence, and production structure. We assemble 2,080 monthly observations for twenty arXiv archives from January 2018 through Au gust 2026 and a separate pseudonymized Mathematics author panel. A regularized convex synthetic control fitted through December 2025 identifies the January August 2026 anomaly, while spatial placebos, prior year pseudo holdouts, donor refits, and alternative preperiods assess comparative robustness. Mathematics recorded 47,127 list entries, 33.5% above 2025 and 11.9% above a synthetic counterfactual of 42,113 entries. Qualified donor and preperiod designs yield 9.6% to 14.9%, and Mathematics has the largest RMSPE ratio among fifteen eligible placebo archives. Subfield growth is broad, with 29 of 30 primary math.* categories expanding. The author panel shows a marked thickening of the repeated output tail. The share of active author units produc ing at least five submissions rose from 2.45% to 3.80%, while the ten submission tail rose from 0.21% to 0.49%. These results document a new and unusually large 2026 Mathematics production regime shift. Its timing and production structure, combined with independent evi dence on AI diffusion and verifiable research tasks, are consistent with delayed diffusion and capability thresh old mechanisms. The comparative design identifies the anomaly, and separate triangulation evaluates AI related explanations. The findings locate verification, selection, and attention as central constraints for research gover nance.
