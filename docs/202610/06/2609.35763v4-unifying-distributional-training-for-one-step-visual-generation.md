## 摘要
分布训练通过在冻结的表示空间中匹配真实特征与生成特征，为一步视觉生成提供集体监督。我们引入了一个统一的理论框架，将分布建模与匹配差异分离，并通过Wasserstein梯度流将全局目标与逐点特征更新连接起来。在此框架下，FD-Loss和高斯核漂移分别通过高斯最优传输和基于核密度的KL匹配恢复。该框架催生了MGFlow，其在可调节的粒度下使用高斯混合模型对特征分布进行建模，介于全局矩和基于样本的表示之间。MGFlow支持最优传输和得分匹配方法，并通过将质量受限的样本分配与成对组件更新相结合来解决混合表达能力无法单独解决的模式崩溃问题。在ImageNet $256\times256$上，MGFlow显著超越FD-Loss基线，在pMF-H和JiT-H上分别达到1.45 $\mathrm{FDr}^6$和1.64的最先进结果。对于文本到图像生成任务，MGFlow将FLUX.2 [klein] 4B进行后训练，转化为一步生成器，在GenEval和PickScore两个指标上均优于原始四步模型。

## Abstract
Distributional training provides collective supervision for one-step visual generation by matching real and generated features in frozen representation spaces. We introduce a unified theoretical framework that separates distribution modeling from matching discrepancy and connects global objectives to pointwise feature updates through Wasserstein gradient flow. Under this framework, FD-Loss and Gaussian-kernel Drifting are recovered through Gaussian optimal transport and kernel-density-based KL matching, respectively. The framework motivates MGFlow, which models feature distributions with Gaussian mixtures at an adjustable granularity between global moments and sample-based representations. MGFlow supports both optimal transport and score-based matching, and couples mass-constrained sample assignment with paired component updates to address mode collapse that mixture expressivity alone does not resolve. On ImageNet $256\times256$, MGFlow substantially surpasses the FD-Loss baseline, achieving state-of-the-art results with 1.45 $\mathrm{FDr}^6$ on pMF-H and 1.64 on JiT-H. For text-to-image generation, MGFlow post-trains FLUX.2 [klein] 4B into a one-step generator that outperforms the original four-step model on both GenEval and PickScore.
