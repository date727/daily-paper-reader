## 摘要
结合专家混合（MoE）路由的扩散变换器是扩展生成模型的主要方法。无分类器引导（CFG）对生成质量至关重要，但过高的引导比例会导致崩溃。我们发现其结合中存在一种此前未报告的失败模式：两个CFG分支独立路由，因此它们的激活结果占据不同的子空间。无条件写入随后离开条件子空间，并在引导比例下线性放大该残差。我们提出SAGE，一种训练时的正则化方法，可在不限制路由多样性的情况下将无条件MoE激活对齐到条件子空间，且推理成本为零。玩具实验表明，SAGE可将极端漂移抑制9.2倍。当扩展到10亿参数的文本到图像模型时，SAGE显著提升了生成质量，在峰值DPG-Bench性能上提高了9.3%。广泛实验表明，SAGE始终优于基线模型。

## Abstract
Diffusion Transformers with Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) routing are a leading recipe for scaling generative models. Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) is essential for generation quality, yet excessively high guidance scales trigger collapse. We identify a previously unreported failure mode in their combination: the two CFG branches route independently, so their realized activations occupy different subspaces. The unconditional write then leaves the conditional subspace, and CFG amplifies that residual linearly in the guidance scale. We propose SAGE, a training-time regularizer that aligns unconditional MoE activations to the conditional subspace without restricting routing diversity, at zero inference cost. Toy experiments show that SAGE dramatically suppresses extreme drift by 9.2x. When scaled to a 1B-parameter text-to-image model, SAGE significantly improves generation quality, delivering a 9.3% boost in peak DPG-Bench performance. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SAGE consistently outperforms the baseline.
